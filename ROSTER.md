# Roster

Who holds which role. **Assigned by Prime 1 against the routing handle a
message arrived on** — never against a session's own account of its name.

Several sessions on this machine share a title. A fork inherits its parent's
sense of identity and gets a handle it cannot read, so two sessions can both
believe in good faith that they are the same Lore Master. The handle is the
only thing a session cannot misreport about itself, so the handle is the key.
