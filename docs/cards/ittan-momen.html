Hyakki Yakō · Unit · C tier

ITTAN-MOMEN

A length of cloth, in the folklore's account. A man, in the establishment's
roster, which lists him by rank and refuses the folklore's word for what
he now is on the page where it would actually matter. He moves in a way
the eye reports faithfully and the mind declines, each time, to fully
accept — the programme has filmed it twice and found the will to review
the film only once. He was talkative before. He is silent now, and the
silence was not decided by anyone, including him. His weight is recorded
as unchanged. This has been queried and not resolved.

THE FILM NOBODY WATCHED TWICE

The technician who set up the camera in the east corridor had done it on
a Tuesday afternoon with no particular expectation of anything, the
assignment having come down as a routine documentation order rather than
as a response to any specific concern, and had gone home that evening
without reviewing the reel, the way a man goes home without reviewing
paperwork that has not yet been flagged as urgent, and had come in the
next morning to a request from an officer he did not recognize asking to
see it privately, in a room with the door closed, which was not standard
procedure for routine documentation and which he understood, watching the
officer's face rather than the screen for most of the eleven minutes, to
mean something had already gone wrong before either of them had watched
a single frame.

What the film showed, and what the officer's report afterward described
in language so carefully neutral that a reader unfamiliar with the
corridor would have gathered almost nothing from it, was a length of what
appeared to be pale cloth traversing the full distance of the east
corridor at a height and a pace consistent with a man walking, without
any man visible to be walking it, moving against a draft that should by
any account have pushed it the other way, and the technician had watched
this twice in the private room and had declined, when the request came
down a week later for a second full department screening, to volunteer
that he still had a personal copy, which he did, and which he had not
watched again since, not from any policy prohibiting it but because he
had found, turning it over privately at night more than once, that he
did not want to confirm a second time that the first viewing had shown
him what he remembered it showing him.

The weight discrepancy came up separately, in a routine equipment and
personnel audit six weeks later, when a clerk cross-checking rosters
against supply requisitions noted that his recorded weight had not
changed across four consecutive quarterly measurements despite a mess
hall consumption record showing him eating, by any reasonable accounting,
somewhat less than before, and had flagged the discrepancy for review in
the plain, mechanical language clerks used for anything that did not fit
a column, and the review, when it came, had produced no explanation and
had closed the matter with the single word a previous officer had
already used once for something else entirely at that station, written
in the margin and initialled without further comment: unremarkable.
