SANITÄTSRAT HELENE AUST

Sector Physician, Hollernbruch. Signs what she is asked to sign, then writes
underneath, in plain German, exactly what it means — and the plain German is
the only part of the file nobody reads.

She does not save units. She reclassifies them. A body scheduled for
disposal filed instead as held pending further assessment buys a day it
was never going to be given. Nineteen objections in eight years, all
received, all forwarded upward, none returned — and every one of them
proves the method that keeps working, works because it is not this one.

THE FULL DAY

The boy on the gurney outside Kadaver's third theatre is not on the list
Aust has been shown, and this is the first thing she notices about him,
before she notices that his chest is moving, slowly, the way a chest moves
when the man attached to it has decided that moving it slowly is safer
than not moving it at all.

He was Muster 21 candidate stock, consulting-unit tissue, until the graft
failed at the third suture and the surgeon wrote non-viable on a form at
eleven that morning and went to lunch, because the form said non-viable
and the form is not required to check. By the schedule pinned to the
corridor board he has been spoilage since eleven, and spoilage goes down
the north stair to Seuche by four, because the stock there improves with
what is fed to it and a fresh body feeds it better than an old one, and
somebody worked that out years ago and wrote it down and nobody has had a
reason to write anything different since.

Aust puts two fingers under his jaw and holds them there for longer than
the pulse requires, working out, in the unhurried way she works out
everything now, which sentence will move him and which will not.

He is alive will not move him. Alive and dead both file the same, once
a form has been signed; the Office has never needed him breathing to use
him and will not stop for it now.

The suture can be redone will not move him either. A second surgeon
this afternoon is a cost against the theatre schedule, and Section Six
has already queried the theatre schedule twice this quarter.

She writes, instead, on a requisition form she carries for exactly this
purpose, that unseasoned stock delivered to Seuche before the full
twenty-four hours shows a measurably poorer take, and that the Office's
own returns from November bear this out, and that holding this specimen
until tomorrow's four o'clock collection is not a mercy, it is a yield
improvement, and yield improvements are the one sentence in this building
that has never once been forwarded upward without an answer.

She signs it Sanitätsrat H. Aust, and underneath, in the margin, in
plain German, she writes the sentence that is not for anyone at
Hollernbruch and never has been, and folds the requisition into thirds so
the margin cannot be read without unfolding it, which nobody at Section
Six has ever had the patience to do.

The orderly reads the top line, nods, and wheels the boy to a holding
room instead of the stair, and marks the log, and goes to his own lunch,
late, and irritated about it, because the theatre schedule this week has
been nothing but corrections.

Aust stands in the corridor for a moment, and then goes to sign the next
one.
