## Draw

Pilots a Gestell 4 — a *walker*, the Allies say, with heavy sarcasm, because
it barely does — and has been stuck in it, literally, twice, and once
figuratively, when the recovery Section VI costed for two crewmen still out
past the treeline came back *declined, pending reassessment*, for the
second time, in the same week Lindt's own frame was pulled free inside four
hours because Pflug went and got him with his own hands and did not fill
out a form first.

He knows exactly what he is owed and to whom, and files nothing about
either, the two debts being, in his own reckoning, not the kind a form
would improve.

## Story

### THE SECOND DECLINE

The frame had gone in to the hip on the Tuesday, a joint seized the way
they always seized once the cold got into the grease, and Lindt had sat in
it for six hours with the war going on somewhere he could hear but not
see, working the release lever that had stopped releasing anything in
1941 and had never been replaced because replacing it would have meant
admitting, in writing, that the release lever did not work, which nobody
at Hollernbruch had yet found a form that permitted.

He had got out on the Wednesday, in the ordinary way — Pflug and two men
from the kennels, in the mud past the treeline, prying the hatch by hand
because the frame's own mechanism for that had also stopped working some
years before Lindt was posted here — and neither of them had said anything
about it since, there being, as far as Lindt could tell, nothing to say
that the getting-out had not already said better.

The two men still past the treeline were a different matter, and Lindt had
learned the shape of that difference on the Thursday, when the recovery
request came back for the second time marked *declined, pending
reassessment*, the same phrase in the same hand as the first decline in
October, and he had gone and found the requisition himself, which was not
his to see and which he read anyway, standing in the corridor outside
Section VI's door with a form he had no authority over, and had worked out,
reading it, that the recovery had been costed against a walker allocation
that had already been spent twice over on frames that did not work, and
that a live crewman, retrieved, cost exactly the same line item as a dead
one recovered for burial, and that the schedule, being a schedule, made no
distinction between the two conditions, only between the cost and the
budget, and the budget had said no both times without ever once being
asked which of the two men were which.

He had not filed a complaint. There was no form for the complaint he
would have written, which was not about the two men specifically, whom he
had met exactly twice and could not have picked out of a photograph, but
about the arithmetic itself — that the same office that had let him out of
a frame in four hours because Pflug carried the cost personally, in his
own hands, off the books, would let two other men stay past a treeline
indefinitely because the honest cost of getting them was written down
somewhere Section VI could read it.

He went back out in the frame on the Friday, the joint reseated and
greased, and worked the lever that did not release anything, and did not
think about the two men more than he could help, this being, he had
decided, the only economy available to him that the Office had not yet
found a way to decline.
