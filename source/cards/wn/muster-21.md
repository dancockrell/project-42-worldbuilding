## Draw

Rare. Kadaver's most expensive product and its strangest — enough left
of the original mind to direct a formation, not enough left to be asked
how. The Office's own paperwork calls it a *consulting unit*, filed
under Personnel rather than Matériel, which is either an oversight
nobody has corrected in six years or the one joke Section Six has ever
permitted itself.

It does not speak in sentences. A raised hand halts a line, two fingers
folded split it, and every man who has taken an order from one learns,
within a week, not to ask what the gesture used to mean before it meant
this.

## Story

### THE HALT SIGNAL

Gefreiter Brandt had served under three formation commanders in two
years and had never before been ordered to a stop by a hand that did not
belong to an officer, or, as far as the paperwork establishing the unit's
rank equivalence went, to a man, and he had asked Werkmeister Voss,
quietly, on his first week attached to the consulting unit's formation,
what he was meant to call it when a report needed a name in the blank
where a commander's name went, and Voss had told him the designation,
Muster 21, third of that model issued, and had told him nothing else,
because there was nothing else in the file Voss was permitted to read
either.

The hand went up at the hedgerow at eleven minutes past six, and the
formation stopped the way it always stopped, without a word passed down
the line, because the order did not travel by word — it travelled by
watching the man in front of you watch the man in front of him, back to
the one figure standing slightly apart at the head of the column,
head tilted at an angle Brandt had stopped finding unusual sometime in his
second month and had never stopped noticing entirely.

Two fingers folded, and the line split without anyone giving the reason,
half into the ditch and half along the treeline, and it was only forty
minutes later, when the machine-gun position they had walked around
opened up on empty hedgerow, that Brandt understood the split had been
correct, the way he had come to understand, without ever once confirming
it against a source that could confirm anything, that the halts and the
splits were always correct, in a proportion no ordinary officer at
Hollernbruch had matched in Brandt's hearing.

He did not ask Voss, that night or after, what a *consulting unit* had
consulted, or with what, or whether the file's word for it — *directs
others* — was doing the same two jobs at once that most words at
Hollernbruch turned out to be doing, because he had watched two other men
ask questions in that formation's first month and neither of them had
been reprimanded for it, which had told him, more clearly than a
reprimand would have, that the question itself was the thing nobody
wanted answered, and Brandt had decided, sometime around the hedgerow,
that a man who is alive because a hand went up at the correct minute has
already been given the only answer he is entitled to.
