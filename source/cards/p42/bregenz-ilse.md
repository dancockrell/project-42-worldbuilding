## Draw

Taken from a laboratory fire in 1922 that burned her papers along with
everything else on the bench, which was, by an accident nobody at Iron
Bell has ever been able to improve on, the cleanest consistency
certification the programme has produced — a chemist erased from her own
field on the same afternoon she was erased from the record that would
otherwise have kept her in it.

Runs the countermeasure work against Seuche and will not say what she
thinks Hollernbruch is actually doing, because she trained close enough to
that world, once, to already know, and knowing is not the same thing as
being willing to say it out loud.

## Story

### CLOSE ENOUGH TO KNOW

Falk asked her directly, once, over a set of cultures neither of them had
gotten to behave the way the countermeasure required, whether Bregenz
thought the Office's work came out of genuine ignorance or something
closer to appetite, a question Falk had clearly been saving for an hour
when the answer might arrive without either of them having to look
directly at it, and Bregenz had set down the pipette she was holding and
not answered for long enough that Falk understood, correctly, that the
silence was itself most of the answer.

She had trained in a city whose university chairs she still, eleven years
on, could name in order of seniority, under men some of whom she had
liked, genuinely, the way a student likes a demanding teacher, and she had
read enough journals from that world before the fire to recognise a shape
when a shape repeated itself at a different address under a different
flag, and recognising it was not a theory she had built at Camp Iron Bell
from insufficient information. It was a memory she had brought with her,
intact, of exactly how such a department justifies its own budget line to
itself, in language that never once uses a word anyone could later be
asked to answer for.

She did not tell Falk this. She did not tell Falk any version of this,
that day or since, not because she doubted Falk would believe her — Falk,
of everyone at the installation, was the one person who would have
believed her without requiring a citation — but because saying it aloud
would have meant explaining how she came to recognise it so quickly, and
that explanation had a shape of its own, with names in it, names of men
she had liked, and she had made a private decision, sometime in the first
year, that those names would not be spoken again in any language a form
could later be filed in.

She went back to the cultures instead, and adjusted the concentration by a
measure so small it barely justified the notation, and told Falk, when
Falk finally asked outright whether the new mixture would hold, that it
was too soon to know, which was true, and which was also, though Falk
never learned this, the only kind of honest answer Bregenz had left
herself the ability to give about anything that traced back far enough.

That evening she took the burned notebook corner out of the drawer where
she kept it, as she did perhaps twice a year, and did not read the two
surviving lines of her own handwriting on it, having long since memorised
them past the point where rereading added anything, and held it instead
the way a person holds a thing to confirm it is still the size it was the
last time, before putting it back exactly where the drawer's own dust
outline said it belonged.
