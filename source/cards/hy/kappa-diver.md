## Draw

Works the water because that is what the fusion made available, and stays
down longer than a man can be down and come back without ceremony — the
programme has costed the capability precisely and the person not at all,
which is its own kind of answer to a question nobody officially asked. He
was a fisherman. He is a fisherman. Both of those are true, in different
and incompatible ways, and he does not like the water any more, and has
told no one, because the men who send him into it would have no use for
that sentence.

## Story

### WHAT THE REPORT COSTED

The report on the harbor mine survey ran to four lines and noted, without
elaboration, an eleven-minute submersion at nine meters in water the
regulation charts listed as unsafe past ninety seconds without apparatus,
and appended nothing further, because the form that generated it had a
line for duration and a line for depth and no line at all for the
question the officer filing it had almost, and then did not, ask him
afterward, which was what eleven minutes down there had actually been
like.

He had been a fisherman off the Noto coast since he was eleven years old,
had known that water the unhurried, total way a man knows a thing he was
never taught and never had to learn, by hours rather than by instruction,
and had loved it in the plain unexamined way you love a thing that has
simply always been there — until the morning the fusion gave him the
water back doubled, tripled, more of everything he had once had freely
and never had to think about, and he had gone under for the first
operational dive expecting to feel, at last, entirely at home, and had
felt instead a cold that had nothing to do with temperature, the specific
wrongness of a room you have lived in your whole life rearranged
overnight by someone who used your own furniture to do it.

What frightened him, surfacing that first time, was not the depth or the
dark or the eleven minutes, which had passed, from inside them, almost
without difficulty — it was that the ease itself no longer felt like his.
A fisherman is proud of what his own lungs and his own years on the water
have earned him. What he had now had been issued, the way a rifle is
issued, calibrated and costed and entered on a form, and he could not
afterward find the seam between the diver he had spent thirty years
becoming and the diver the folklore had simply installed, and the not-
knowing sat in him worse than any of the actual water had.

He surfaced now the way he always surfaced, level and unhurried, and
climbed the ladder, and reported the mooring line clear, in the flat
correct voice the report expected of him, and said nothing about the cold
that was not temperature, because there was no officer on that dock, in
three years, who had ever once asked him a question with room in it for
the answer he would have had to give.
