## Draw

Fused with something the folklore describes in four incompatible ways at
once, and the programme's file uses the folklore's word for lack of one
of its own, because whatever came in did not consult the taxonomy before
arriving. He is not a chimera — a chimera is a fact about a body, and
what is wrong here is not in the body. He is a man, and it is using him.
The record calls it a successful fusion and defines neither term. Nobody
has asked him what it feels like. The form, when he goes looking for the
line, has no box.

## Story

### THE LINE THE FORM DOES NOT HAVE

The intake clerk had four reference volumes open on the desk when he sat
down for the quarterly assessment, each one offering a different account
of what the folklore called him — beast of ill omen in one, a warning
cry in another, a shape assembled from parts that belonged to four
different animals in a third, and in the fourth, the oldest of the
volumes, simply a darkness that arrives before something worse and is
sometimes mistaken for the worse thing itself — and the clerk had read
all four aloud to him, not unkindly, in the tone of a man hoping the
subject might recognize himself in one of them and settle the question
from the inside, and he had listened to all four and recognized nothing,
because none of them had been written with the actual experience in mind,
only with the outside of it, the shape a frightened observer might report
centuries after the fact to somebody who was never going to check.

He had tried, the first few assessments, to describe what it was
actually like from within, and had watched the clerk's pen stop moving
each time, not from disinterest but from the plain mechanical fact that
the intake form had a box for symptom, a box for capability, and a box
for operational notes, and nothing he said fit cleanly into any of the
three, so that his answers were either shortened until they fit or left
off the record entirely, and he had stopped trying by the third quarter,
not because the experience had grown easier to bear but because
describing it accurately had never once, in three tries, produced
anything the form could hold.

What he had settled into instead, in the eighteen months since, was a
private arithmetic of his own devising, unrecorded anywhere, by which he
tracked the difference between the days it felt like something riding
alongside him and the days it felt like something that had simply
replaced the part of him that used to notice riders — and he had come to
dread the second kind more than the first, though he had never found
language sturdy enough to explain to anyone, including himself on the
better days, exactly why one frightened him and the other merely
exhausted him.

The clerk closed the fourth volume, wrote *successful fusion,
classification pending further review* in the space provided, and thanked
him for his patience, and he said that he was welcome, in the flat
correct voice the form expected, and left the intake room the way he
always left it: no better understood than he had arrived, by either of
them, and no closer to a box that would hold what he actually knew.
