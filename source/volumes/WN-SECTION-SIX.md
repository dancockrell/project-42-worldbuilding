# WERK NACHTIGALL — SECTION VI

**Fork B.** Accounts and Estimates. The most powerful body in the faction and the
one with no face.

---

## 1. What it actually is

**Section VI (Accounts and Estimates)**, second floor of the main building, in
what was the sanatorium's children's ward. **Six people work there.**

Everyone at Hollernbruch says *Section Six* as though it were a single person.
Nobody can name any of the six. Personnel who have worked on the site for a
decade could not tell you what any of them look like, and have never found this
strange.

**They are not sinister. They are accountants**, and they are good at it, and
that is worse.

---

## 2. The mechanism of its power

Section VI does not decide what is done. **It decides what things are filed as**,
and on this site those are the same act.

- **Procedure Seuche is agricultural research.** Reclassifying it as medical
  research would attach the disclosure requirements that follow medical research.
  Section VI has declined reclassification twice, in writing, on grounds that the
  current filing is *adequate to purpose*.
- **The Bestiarium kennels are livestock.** Which is why a veterinary officer
  with no clearance inspects them annually and files a form about feed.
- **Kadaver's consumption is surgical consumables.**

**Nobody at Hollernbruch has ever been overruled by a general.** Several have
been overruled by a clerk with a query about depreciation.

### The two rules that make it frightening

**It never says no.** It says *approved in part*, or *held pending
clarification*, or *refers you to the schedule*. A flat refusal could be
appealed. **A partial approval cannot**, because it is not a refusal, and there
is nothing to appeal against.

**It never explains.** Not from secrecy — from **form design.** The reply
templates have no field for reasoning. Somebody would have to write in the
margin, and nobody writes in the margin.

---

## 3. Why it is not a villain, and must never be written as one

Rule 7 applies with unusual force here.

**Section VI has no opinion about what Hollernbruch does.** It has never
expressed one. It does not approve of the Office and does not disapprove; it
administers an appropriation, and it administers it competently, and it would
administer a harbour dredging programme to the same standard.

That is the point. **The moment Section VI is written as malevolent it becomes
comprehensible, and comprehensible is comfortable.** An accounts department that
*wants* the horror is a cartoon. An accounts department that has genuinely never
thought about it — that finds the whole file mildly tedious and slightly
irritating on the feed returns — is the actual thing.

**Menace is supplied by the reader.** A Section VI letter that sounds threatening
has been written wrong.

---

## 4. The six, as far as anyone knows

Deliberately thin. **Do not name them.** They are known, when known at all, by
what they sign.

- **The one who signs the feed returns.** Since 1939. Has never visited the
  kennels. Pflug has corresponded with this person for five years and does not
  know their name, rank, sex, or age.
- **The one who handles Seuche.** Declined reclassification twice. Both letters
  are three sentences long.
- **The one who queries transport.** Objected to the March 1943 transport on
  cost — **the only objection anyone raised to it in the entire Office**, and it
  was about the money.
- **Three others.** Nobody has established what they do.

**The March 1943 detail is the best thing in this file.** The only person in Werk
Nachtigall who tried to stop the thing that drives the story did it on a cost
line and would not recognise the description.

---

## 5. Voice — worked samples

The register: **procurement, mild irritation, and total finality.**

> Your requisition for four hundred (400) muzzles is approved in part. Two
> hundred are released against the current quarter. The remainder is held pending
> clarification of the discrepancy between your stated establishment and your feed
> returns, which do not reconcile.

> Section VI notes that the discrepancy has been favourable in every quarter
> since 1941 and would welcome an explanation it can file.

> The occupant is entered on the establishment as present. Section VI has queried
> whether he should be entered as *present* or as *equipment in the field*, since
> the two are drawn against different appropriations, and requests guidance
> before the next return.

> Section VI regrets that the allocation cannot be shared as proposed and refers
> the ministry to the schedule.

> The Director's submission is acknowledged. Section VI refers the Director to
> the schedule.

**That last one is the whole department in nine words**, and it is addressed to
the man who runs the site.

## 6. Its edges

From the hub network:

- `section_six -> brehm_sandt_klaus COMMAND 0.7` — not on any org chart.
- `section_six -> pflug_otto COMMAND 0.6` — **they have never met.** The feed
  returns are the entire relationship.
- `brehm_sandt_klaus -> section_six FEAR 0.5` — **the Director is afraid of
  Accounts**, which is correct, and he would not put it that way.
- `section_six -> the_march_subject KNOWLEDGE 0.2` — knows only the transport
  cost, which was the objection.

**Note what is absent: Section VI has no REGARD edges in either direction.** Not
zero — none. Nobody regards them and they regard nobody, and that is what an
institution rather than a workplace looks like on a graph.

## 7. Open, deliberately

- Whether any of the six has ever read what they are funding.
- What Section VI would do if the Office asked it to stop. **Never answered.**
- Whether the transport objection was only about cost. **Never answered, and it
  is the most tempting question in the faction.**
