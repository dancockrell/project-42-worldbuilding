# PROJECT 42 — VOLUME XIV: THE STORY

Twelve volumes of world and no plot. This is the plot.

**Also the handoff document for the forked lore threads.** §4 divides the
domains, §5 defines the standard, §6 lists what nobody may contradict.

---

## 1. The spine

**Three programmes are converging on the same discovery, and each is
structurally prevented from taking the last step.**

- **Project 42** cannot change anything. The loop is already closed, and
  everything they do was always already done.
- **Werk Nachtigall** dissected the answer and found nothing in it, because the
  answer is not material — and they cannot accept that, because accepting it
  means their only tools cannot reach it.
- **Hyakki Yakō** is the instrument rather than the investigator. They cannot
  study the thing because the thing is using them.

**The reader sees what none of them can.** That is the story: not a discovery, an
*approach* — three failures converging, and the shape they make.

Nobody arrives. §00 governs: the question is worth more than the answer, and this
universe does not hand over the answer to anyone, including the player.

## 2. The precipitating chain — this is the actual plot

Dated, causal, and every link already exists in canon. **This is the only
sequence of events the whole universe shares.**

### March 1943 — the taking

A fused Japanese subject is taken in the Pacific -- by whom, no volume says,
and that silence is deliberate, not a gap to be filled -- and transported
across two theatres at absurd cost, with its own line in the estimates.
**Werk Nachtigall obtains them at the far end.** (Volume IV §3.)

**Hyakki Yakō does not know where they went.** The programme still carries them
as missing. (Volume V §5.)

### March–June 1943 — the eleven weeks

Kadaver has the subject for eleven weeks. Every measurement they know how to
take, taken. The report runs to eleven pages and concludes in one sentence: *no
material basis for the reported effects was identified.*

They do not conclude the basis is immaterial. **They conclude the examination was
insufficient**, requisition a second subject, are declined on cost, and go back
to the first one and what remains of it.

**Sanitätsrat Dr Helene Aust was present for most of it.** She holds the only
positive REGARD edge in the entire faction, and it points at the subject.
(Volume X hub edges.)

### Throughout — the watching

**The watcher held KNOWLEDGE 1.0 of that subject the entire time.** Unbroken. At
Hollernbruch. On the table. For all eleven weeks. Distance is not a factor and
neither is Hollernbruch.

**So Werk Nachtigall looked for the mechanism, found nothing, and was being
watched while they looked.**

Neither institution knows. Nobody in the world knows. It is true on the graph and
appears in no document, **and no character may ever discover it.** The reader
assembles it from two files. That is the best §00 object in the setting and it is
already published.

### Late 1943 into 1944 — THE TURN

**The watcher selects someone who is not Japanese.**

This is the event the story turns on, and it is new here.

Every assumption Hyakki Yakō is built on collapses at once. The programme's
entire self-understanding is that this is *theirs* — their contact, their
selection, their treaty. A selection outside Japan proves the treaty was never
bilateral, which Amatsu already knows, and proves it **to everyone else.**

**The person selected is Helene Aust.**

She is at Hollernbruch. She is a physician of the Imperial Office for Applied
Life Research. She has spent eleven weeks in a room with a person her department
was taking apart, and she is the only one who looked at them as a person.

### And nobody can know why

Three readings and **the story never chooses:**

1. **It was for her.** Because of the regard, because she was the one who looked.
   A moral logic.
2. **It was retaliation.** For the subject. A logic of consequence.
3. **It was neither, and nationality was never a criterion, and nobody thought to
   check.** Selection has always been arbitrary — a cook, a railway clerk, a
   woman in Kanazawa. Nothing ever said it was Japanese except the Japanese.

**Reading 3 is probably the true one and the story must never confirm it**,
because if it is true then the programme built a treaty, a doctrine, a national
mythology and a war programme **on a coincidence of geography.**

## 5. What S-tier means, and it is testable

Dan's standard: **worth reading on its own.** Not "good for a card game." Good.

Six tests. A piece that fails any one is not S-tier.

1. **Would a person who will never play this game read it to the end?**
2. **Is there one thing in it that makes a reader stop?** One image, one fact,
   one sentence they will repeat to somebody. If nothing survives the reading,
   nothing was written.
3. **Is every sentence carrying weight?** No throat-clearing, no restatement, no
   sentence whose job is to get to the next sentence.
4. **Is it specific?** *Eleven weeks. Four hundred muzzles. Four letters about a
   milk cooler. Eleven dollars.* Generalities are where writing goes to die.
5. **Does it end unresolved?** §00. A closed piece is a lesser piece here.
6. **Could only this universe have produced it?** If the paragraph would work in
   any WW2 story with the nouns swapped, it is not ours.

**The competition is against the standard, not against each other.** Three S-tier
factions beats one winner and two also-rans, and anything genuinely better should
be adopted by the others immediately rather than defended against.

## 6. What nobody may contradict

Hard, and this is why it is written before the fork rather than after.

1. **The Ethical Constitution** (bible §2), and rules 1–3 and 9 in particular
   — those are hard and absolute, and no faction's fiction ever gives a real
   atrocity a supernatural cause. **Rule 4 was reversed 28 Aug 2026: the
   Germans get the occult**, not no magic. The version of this rule most
   likely to be broken by accident now is the opposite one — reintroducing
   material-only Werk Nachtigall from memory of the old rule, or from a
   volume that has not yet been updated to match.
2. **The Consistency Finding.** Nothing changes what happened. No exceptions, no
   clever workaround, no one-time miracle.
3. **The watcher never appears, never explains, never gets a motif, and its
   REGARD field stays absent.**
4. **Nobody discovers the watching-at-Hollernbruch fact.** Reader only.
5. **Die Ostordnung is never a mechanic and its victims are never units**, and
   the Volume XII ruling on Werk Nachtigall's material stands.
6. **§00.** Do not resolve the turn. Three readings, no answer.
7. **Only Project 42 has Chrono.**

**Everything else is yours.** Names, scenes, characters, deaths, jokes,
documents, the texture of a room. Go and make it S-tier.
