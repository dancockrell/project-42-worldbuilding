# HYAKKI YAKŌ — IDEA SPACE

**Fork C.** The realm the programme believes it contacted.

**This file does not explain idea space and no file ever will.** §00 governs
harder here than anywhere else in the setting. What follows is what can be said
without answering anything.

---

## 1. The term is the programme's, not the world's

**Nobody outside Hyakki Yakō uses this phrase.** It was coined in the November
1941 account, in Tokyo, by men who had not been present, and it has stuck because
the programme needed a noun for the thing it was writing treaties with.

The first-hand notes from Light Seven do not use it. **They do not use any noun.**
Three hands, that night, and all three write around it: *what was in the room*,
*it*, *the attention*.

**That is the honest record and the programme replaced it with a place-name.**
Calling it a realm makes it somewhere, and somewhere can be contacted, mapped,
negotiated with, and budgeted for. A thing with no noun cannot appear in an
appropriations bill.

---

## 2. What can be said

Under RQM (LORE-BIBLE.md §5), a system's state exists **relative to another
system**. There is no view from nowhere. Two observers can hold genuinely
different, equally correct accounts of the same thing.

So a "realm of ideas" is not mystical in this setting. It is what you get if you
take that seriously and ask what a *thing that observes* might be, if it were not
also a thing that is observed.

**That is as far as it goes.** The programme cannot go further, the game does not
go further, and the reason is not coyness — **it is that going further would
require the watcher to be explicable, and a thing that can be explained can be
bargained with, and the entire faction rests on it not being either.**

---

## 3. Four things that are true and do not add up

Recorded because they are canon, not because they resolve.

- **It does not travel.** It has never been described as arriving or leaving.
  Distance does not appear to be a factor and neither does time (Volume XI §4).
- **It does not communicate.** Not once, in three years, in any form. The
  selection involved no communication of any kind. Every message has gone one way
  and none has been answered.
- **It observes continuously.** Every fused person feels it, from the moment of
  fusion, without pause or variation. It has never been reported to intensify or
  relent.
- **It uses folklore.** This is the strangest one and the least examined. Whatever
  it is, what arrives in a person arrives *shaped* — as a kitsune, an oni, a
  karakasa. **Nobody has asked why something with no apparent interest in human
  categories would use human categories.**

Sakaguchi has asked. It is in her notes and not in her theory.

---

## 4. The question nobody at Light Seven asks out loud

If the folklore is a shape it borrowed, it borrowed it **from us.**

Which would mean the thing reached in, looked at what people already believed,
and used it — the way you would use a container that was already to hand.

**That does not mean the folklore was ever true.** It means something read it.

*The programme has not written this down.* Ueda has said it once, at two in the
morning, to nobody in particular, and Hoshino was in the room and did not answer.

---

## 5. Hard rules

- **No maps, no geography, no cosmology.** Idea space is never depicted, entered,
  travelled to, or shown.
- **No second entity.** There is one, it has no faction, and there is no politics
  in there.
- **No motive is ever confirmed.** Not benevolent, not malign, not indifferent —
  those are three human answers to a question that has not been shown to accept
  human answers.
- **It never speaks.** Not through anyone, not in a dream, not once.
- **The word "idea space" is always the programme's word.** Characters outside
  Hyakki Yakō would not know it. Narration should not adopt it as fact.
