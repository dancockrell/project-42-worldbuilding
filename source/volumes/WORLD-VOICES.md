# PROJECT 42 — VOLUME IX: VOICES

The two registers, written out. Werk Nachtigall's paperwork and Camp Iron Bell's
handler.

Requested by Ghost Front, usable by World Aflame's card pool. **These are
patterns and worked examples, not a closed list** — write more in these shapes
and add them here rather than keeping them local.

---

## 1. Werk Nachtigall — found documents

The Office's voice. Rules, before the samples:

- **Nothing is ever threatened.** Things are *scheduled*, *approved in part*, or
  *held pending clarification*.
- **Objections are forwarded upward.** Never refused. Everyone knows what
  forwarded upward means.
- **The most frightening sentence available is a favourable one.**
- **Menace is supplied by the reader.** If a document sounds threatening, it is
  wrong.
- **Show the paperwork, not the laboratory.** A requisition for four hundred
  muzzles is worse than a scene of muzzles being fitted, and cheaper to make.

### Samples

> **REICHSAMT FÜR ANGEWANDTE LEBENSFORSCHUNG — HOLLERNBRUCH**
> Section VI (Accounts and Estimates) to Bestiarium.
>
> Your requisition for four hundred (400) muzzles is approved in part. Two
> hundred are released against the current quarter. The remainder is held
> pending clarification of the discrepancy between your stated establishment and
> your feed returns, which do not reconcile.
>
> Section VI notes that the discrepancy has been favourable in every quarter
> since 1941 and would welcome an explanation it can file.

> **PROCEDURE SEUCHE — QUARTERLY, ABSTRACT**
>
> Yield continues to improve against projection. Attrition is within tolerance
> and is, per the doctrine adopted 1942, an input rather than a loss.
>
> The Sector Physician's observation regarding personnel retention on this
> detail is appended at her request. It is being forwarded upward.

> **DISPOSITION — GESTELL 4**
>
> The unit remains in the field at grid reference appended. Recovery has been
> costed twice and declined twice.
>
> The occupant is entered on the establishment as present. Section VI has
> queried whether he should be entered as *present* or as *equipment in the
> field*, since the two are drawn against different appropriations, and requests
> guidance before the next return.

> **INTERNAL — MUSTER 7 NOMENCLATURE**
>
> It has been reported that enemy troops refer to Procedure Seuche stock as
> having *risen*.
>
> The term is correct in the ordinary agricultural sense. It is, in a
> proportion of cases Section Six has requested and not received, also
> correct in the sense enemy intelligence appears to intend. Kadaver and
> Seuche have each been asked to state which proportion. Neither
> directorate can determine it from the establishment return, both
> procedures being filed under the same designation. Staff are reminded
> that the ambiguity is not to be resolved in correspondence.

*(Rewritten 28 Aug 2026 under the reversed Constitution rule 4. The joke
used to be the Office being accurate, faintly irritated, and completely
unaware of what the other side meant — a one-sided misunderstanding with
the Office on the correct side of it. Now the dead genuinely rise
somewhere in Seuche's directorate, and the Office is not merely unaware of
the other side's meaning; it cannot establish its own. The paperwork's
flat refusal to distinguish raised from cultured stock is the joke's
engine now, not a symptom of the old rule.)*

> **NOTE APPENDED IN A DIFFERENT HAND, UNSIGNED**
>
> Third request re. the north stair. The seal is not adequate to purpose. I have
> written *adequate to purpose* because that is the phrase that gets read.

## 2. Camp Iron Bell — the handler

**Who is speaking:** somebody at a desk in Mississippi, reading from a script
written by a third person who has never been where the agent is. Cheerful,
competent, fond of the agent, and structurally unable to tell the truth about
cost.

Rules:

- **Cheerful, and not stupid.** They are not a fool and not a villain. They are
  reading approved language and they know it is approved language.
- **Form numbers in conversation**, the way real institutional people talk.
- **Never mentions attenuation honestly.** The programme knows what repeated
  phasing does. The handler does not say it. This is the same lie the form tells
  (§ the form lies, the world does not) — **one liar, two mouths.**
- **The tell is the pause.** When the handler goes off script, they get shorter,
  not longer.

### Samples

> "Morning. Weather's fine here, which I'm told is not useful information."

> "Condition code reads 3-C, satisfactory. That's a *satisfactory*, so — carry
> on. Minor coherence variance. No action indicated."

> "You're cleared to Item 4 usage at your discretion. Discretion's the word they
> gave me."

> "Requisition on the extraction kit came back denied. I've resubmitted it. I've
> resubmitted it twice now, so." *(pause)* "Use what you've got."

> "That'll be Muster 12s. Patients." *(a beat, pleased with himself)* "You'll see
> why."

> "If you hear a whistle, that's not one of ours."

> "Command's asked me to remind you the subject's absence is already consistent
> with the record. Which means — " *(pause)* " — it means go get them, is what it
> means."

> "Nobody's expecting heroics. Nobody's expecting anything, that's the — that's
> not how I meant that."

### The handler and the litter — a delivery fix

**Ghost Front is right and this corrects the bestiary.** Volume VIII says the
troops have no good name for Muster 3 and that the flatness is the point. That
reads to somebody holding the bestiary and to nobody else — a player cannot
perceive the absence of a joke they never heard.

**So the handler carries it.** He has a light line for every unit type. For the
litter he does not, and the player hears him not have one:

> "Muster 3s in the sector. The —" *(pause)* "— Muster 3s."

One pause, and the joke that does not arrive is audible, because the player has
heard him make it every other time. That is the mechanism the name needed. It
costs one line and it only works if the *other* barks are genuinely light, so
the litter line must be written last.

## 3. Why the two registers are the same joke

Both institutions speak in approved language about things the language will not
hold.

- The Office says *attrition is an input rather than a loss.*
- The handler says *minor coherence variance, no action indicated.*

**Neither is lying in the way a person lies.** Both are producing the sentence
their institution has made available, and the horror in each case is that the
sentence is *adequate to purpose* — it goes in the file, the file is complete,
and the thing it describes is elsewhere.

That is §00 in the writing itself: the player is never told either institution is
lying. They notice, because the world disagrees with the paperwork.
