# PROJECT 42 — VOLUME VI: THE TIMELINE

How this world got to 1944, and what is different from ours.

**Real history is marked ▪. Our divergence is marked ◆.** That distinction is not
decoration — it is the sourcing discipline of §0a applied to the timeline. If you
are about to write a scene that depends on a fact, check which mark it carries,
and if it carries ▪ then check it properly before shipping.

Several structural decisions below are **loremaster calls** rather than
consequences anyone could derive. They are marked and Dan can overrule any of
them.

---

## Before the divergence — all real

▪ **1871.** The German Empire is proclaimed at Versailles. Wilhelm I, Kaiser.

▪ **1884–85.** The Berlin Conference partitions Africa among European powers.
Germany takes South West Africa, East Africa, Kamerun, Togoland.

▪ **1888.** Wilhelm II takes the throne, aged 29.

▪ **1891.** The Pan-German League is founded. Its programme — eastward
settlement, racial hierarchy, colonial expansion as national necessity — is
public, respectable, and has parliamentary support. **Every idea later attributed
to a single Austrian demagogue is already in circulation, in print, under the
Kaiser, forty years early.** This is the fact the entire setting rests on.

▪ **1904–1908. The Herero and Nama genocide, German South West Africa.**
Lothar von Trotha's extermination order. Survivors driven into the Omaheke to die
of thirst. Concentration camps, including Shark Island, where prisoners were
worked to death and where skulls were cleaned by surviving prisoners and shipped
to Berlin to be measured. Roughly 80% of the Herero and 50% of the Nama killed.
Generally identified as the first genocide of the twentieth century.

▪ **1908 onward.** Eugen Fischer, who studied those skulls and conducted racial
research in the colony, builds an academic career on it. He will later found and
direct the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Anthropology, Human Heredity and
Eugenics. His protégé Otmar von Verschuer succeeds him as director in 1942 --
and it is to Verschuer, at that institute, that Josef Mengele, his direct
student, sends specimens from Auschwitz.

▪ **1914–1918.** The Great War. In the east, the German military administration
**Ober Ost** governs occupied territory as a racialised colonial regime — a
laboratory for administering conquered populations, run inside Europe.

▪ **1917.** Revolution in Russia. Germany assists Lenin's return. The Bolsheviks
take power.

## ◆ The divergence — November 1918

**In our history:** Germany collapses, the Kaiser abdicates and flees to the
Netherlands, the Republic is proclaimed, and Versailles imposes terms that a
movement will spend fifteen years promising to avenge.

**◆ Here, the Empire does not fall.** The war ends in an exhausted negotiated
peace rather than a collapse — no abdication, no imposed treaty, no humiliation
to avenge, no stab-in-the-back myth, no Republic, and **no vacuum for anyone to
fill.**

This is the whole argument of the setting, and it is worth stating plainly:
**every single condition historians cite as the cause of Nazism has been
removed.** No Versailles. No hyperinflation-as-national-grievance. No Weimar. No
Hitler — he remains an unremarkable veteran and dies unrecorded.

And it arrives in the same place anyway, because the machinery was never his. It
was the Pan-German League's, and the colonial ministry's, and the university
chairs'. He inherited it. Here, nobody needs to.

*(Loremaster call: Austria-Hungary and the Ottoman Empire still fragment — their
collapses were internal and would not have been prevented by a German negotiated
peace. Germany emerges as the only intact continental empire, which is more
dangerous than victory would have been, because it looks like restraint.)*

## ◆ The Empire that did not have to hide

**◆ 1919–1930.** Never defeated, the Empire never conceals anything. Race science
stays in the universities: funded, chaired, peer-reviewed, unbroken from the
colonial skull measurements. Colonial administrative practice comes home. There
is no revolutionary party because the state never lost the thread and no one
needs to seize what they already hold.

▪ **1929 onward.** The Depression is global and arrives here too. *(Loremaster
call: it strengthens rather than destabilises the Empire — a state that has not
been humiliated meets an economic crisis as a management problem, and expands its
apparatus to meet it. The tools built to administer a depression are the tools
that later administer something else.)*

**◆ 1931. The Reichsamt für Angewandte Lebensforschung is established** and takes
the Hollernbruch Sanatorium. A department, with a budget line. See Volume IV.

**◆ 1934. Die Ostordnung** — the Eastern Ordering — is formalised as policy.
Settlement, clearance, and administration of the east, descended in a straight
documented line from Ober Ost and from the colonial ministry. It is not a secret.
It is *policy*, debated and funded, with an appropriations history.

**This is the Holocaust's place in this world.** Not called that, not done by
Nazis, not at Auschwitz — those things do not exist here. Done instead by the
same state that ran Shark Island, thirty years on, with better rail. *The same
bad things, differently.*

## The war

▪ **1931.** Japan takes Manchuria.
▪ **1937.** Full-scale war in China. **Unit 731 begins operating.**

**◆ 1939.** The Empire moves east in force. Not revanchism — it has nothing to
avenge — but the Pan-German programme executed on schedule by a government that
has always said this is what it intended. Britain and France declare war.

*(Loremaster call: this is a harder war to explain to a public than one framed as
righting a wrong, and the Empire does not especially try. It is presented as
administration. That absence of hysteria is the point: no rallies, no screaming,
just an appropriations bill and a timetable.)*

▪ **1941.** Japan attacks Pearl Harbor. The United States enters.

**◆ August 1941.** At a research station on the Noto peninsula, something looks
at eleven people and selects four. The **Hyakki Yakō** programme begins, though
it will not be called that or understood as such until November. See Volume V.

**◆ January 1943.** Nikola Tesla dies in the Hotel New Yorker, in debt. His
papers are seized by the Office of Alien Property — ▪ both real. **◆ Here, they
reach Camp Iron Bell inside a fortnight.**

**◆ March 1943.** Camp Iron Bell (Signal Corps Training Annex) is established in
Copiah County, Mississippi. See Volume III.

**◆ March 1943.** Werk Nachtigall obtains a fused Japanese subject and finds
nothing in it. See Volume IV §3.

**◆ November 1943.** Frame Two kills Sergeant Aurelio Fitch. The first retrieval
closes on Frame Three the following month.

*Not an error, and do not “correct” it. Four people are already on the
roster with retrieval dates from March to November 1943, all of them through
Frame Three. December is when Frame Three begins working in the ordinary
causal sense — Osei arrives in November through it, and six weeks later
solves the closure problem that makes it work. The earlier retrievals are the
ones it had already done. That is what Volume II means by *the one that
already worked*, and why nobody at the camp says it out loud.*

## ◆ Where 1944 stands

The war is genuinely in the balance and no one has a decisive advantage, because
**all three super-programmes are producing less than their budgets claim.**

- **Project 42** has retrieved a small number of people at ruinous cost and
  believes it is steering history. It is not (the Consistency Finding). It cannot
  reach anyone history was careful about, which means it cannot reach anyone who
  matters in the way it wants.
- **Werk Nachtigall** has real output — the surgical wing works, the cultures
  work — and none of it is elegant, and the thing it actually wants is not
  obtainable with a scalpel.
- **Hyakki Yakō** has perhaps forty fused subjects, no control over selection, no
  understanding of the mechanism, and a treaty nothing signed.

**Nobody is winning the secret war. Everybody's paperwork says otherwise.**

Meanwhile the ordinary war — infantry, logistics, bombing, ships — continues at
full scale and is what will actually decide it, which none of the three
programmes can say out loud without ending their own funding.

## What the games sit on

- **World Aflame** — 1944, the front, all three factions in contact. The card
  pool is the ordinary war with three extraordinary programmes bolted to it.
- **Ghost Front** — a Project 42 retrieval agent deploying from Camp Iron Bell.
  Named for ▪ the real late-1944 nickname for the quiet Ardennes sector.
- **The Long Night** — the Chrono pillar, reaching into other eras. Camp Iron
  Bell for orientation, then the Victorian period and beyond. Every era it visits
  is bound by the Consistency Finding: the programme can only take people the
  record already loses, and in 1888 Whitechapel that is a very particular and
  very uncomfortable category of person.
