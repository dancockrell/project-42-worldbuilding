# Lore pages — three worked samples

Tonal reference for the one-page unit and situation bios. Every card gets one
eventually; these three establish the voice per faction and the document-format
system in LORE-BIBLE.md §8.

**Cut against the bible, 27 Aug 2026; the Werk Nachtigall sample rewritten
again 28 Aug 2026 under the reversed Constitution rule 4.** The original
drafts were written by the world-aflame-godot mechanics session and are
theirs; this is a re-cut, not a first draft. They predated the bible and
were stale in two ways: it used "Section 43" for the Allied site (now Camp
Iron Bell), fixed in the first cut, and it kept Werk Nachtigall's sample
deliberately material-only under the old rule 4. **That rule was reversed
28 Aug 2026 — the Germans get the occult — so the sample below now leans
into it**, in the same flat bureaucratic register as before. Live document,
not a historical record: other card writers copy tone from these pages.
`tools/lore-check.py` covers the card pool; these long-form pages are
reviewed by hand against §2.

Each page is **a document that exists in the world**, not narration about the
world. That is the whole system: story arrives as evidence.

## PROJECT 42 — Overclocked Current Warden
*Branch: Current. Effect: +5 Power per card in hand.*
*Document format: personnel file, Camp Iron Bell, partially redacted.*

> **INCIDENT REPORT 42-C-118.** Filed under *training exercise*. Reopened three
> times. Closed three times.

Corporal Adaeze Bright arrived at Camp Iron Bell with a physics degree she was
not supposed to have yet. The retrieval team's paperwork says she enlisted
normally in '42, like everyone else, because that is what the paperwork says
about all of them. Nobody asks the settled where they learned what they know.
You just notice that Bright reads a live wire the way other people read
weather.

Her file lists one commendation and one court-martial, both for the same
afternoon. The commendation is for holding a position. The court-martial is for
how. Witnesses agree she never fired a shot — she stood there with current
arcing hand to hand between four soldiers she had wired together like she was
the outlet, and the enemy line broke, because nobody wants to walk into that.
The report calls it *unauthorised use of personnel as conductive medium.*
Bright calls it Tuesday.

There is a second file. It is shorter. It concerns the base commander's
standing order that Corporal Bright be billeted in the separate quarters
required by the state of Mississippi, and Bright's written request — denied,
twice, on grounds of local ordinance — to be billeted with the four men she is
wired to. The programme's own regulations do not care where anyone is from. The
county does. The programme has not found this contradiction interesting enough
to write down anywhere except here.

She keeps every requisition slip she has ever been denied. Says it is for the
memoir. Command suspects it is a battery.

> *Internal art — a Polaroid clipped to the file corner: Bright mid-arc, four
> silhouetted soldiers lit from between their joined hands, every face turned
> from the flash except hers.*

## WERK NACHTIGALL — Verwesend Pesthund
*Branch: Seuche. Effect: scales with cards discarded.*
*Document format: interdepartmental correspondence, water-damaged.*

> **To the Quartermaster-General, Northern Sector.** Copy to Accounts.
> **Do not file with the healthy kennels.**

The Pesthund line stopped being about dogs somewhere around the third
generation, and stopped being about the culture somewhere around the
fifth. What comes out of the beds answers a whistle and does not breathe
between commands. The veterinary officer notes, without further comment,
that several specimens predate their own feed returns by a margin the
returns cannot account for. The occult section's involvement in this
line is recorded under Bau. B/36 as *consultation, general* — the only
line item any of them has ever been given, on this or any procedure.

The doctrine is now a line item either way. They improve as the kennel is
spent, so the kennel is no longer fed meat; the kennel is fed itself,
living or otherwise, and what is standing by spring is worth ten of what
you started with. Attached please find the cost variance, which is
favourable regardless of which procedure produced it. Attached also, at
the Sector Physician's insistence, is the note that the men detailed to
that duty do not re-enlist for it, and that several have asked, off the
record, whether the ones that do not breathe are meant to be there. The
Physician has asked twice that this be forwarded upward. It is being
forwarded upward.

Procedure Seuche remains filed under agricultural research. Accounts has
declined to reclassify it on the grounds that reclassification would
require the disclosures attached to medical research, or the disclosures
attached to a different research entirely, and the current filing is, in
Accounts' phrase, *adequate to purpose.*

His Imperial Majesty's government has not commented on the smell, or on
the second smell.

> *Internal art — a kennel gate at dusk, hinges bloomed and soft, one pale
> shape just past the light, standing too still for something breathing
> and moving too well for something that has stopped. More silhouette
> than animal. Patient in a way that reads worse than aggressive.*

## HYAKKI YAKŌ — Kuroi Oni-Drummer
*Branch: Oni. Effect: destroys itself to strengthen others.*
*Document format: a letter, never sent.*

> To whoever finds this, if there is a whoever left to find it —

I do not remember agreeing to the drum.

I remember volunteering for the unit. I remember the ceremony. Between those
two memories there is a gap the shape of a held breath, and I have never been
able to fill it back in. What I have instead is the drum, and the certainty —
not a fear, a certainty — that something is keeping time with me from a
direction I cannot point at.

They tell us the fusion chooses its own reasons. Mine apparently wanted a
drummer. I have become very good at reading which rhythm makes my friends fight
harder, and which rhythm means I should stop, because after that one there will
not be enough of me left to need the drum back.

Captain Ishida says the treaty protects us. I have read the treaty. It is
written in our hand, on our paper, and filed in our cabinet, and nothing in it
was ever signed by the other party. We wrote down what we hoped had been agreed
and then put it somewhere official, the way you do. I do not think it has read
it. I do not think it files things.

I do not know what it wants when it is finished with us. I know it is patient.
I know it is listening to this letter being written, the way it listens to
everything, and that when I put the pen down it will still be

> *Internal art — a drum alone on scorched ground, skin still faintly moving.
> No drummer. A long shadow behind it that does not match anything standing
> nearby.*
