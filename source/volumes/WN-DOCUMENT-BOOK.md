# WERK NACHTIGALL — THE DOCUMENT BOOK

**Fork B.** Found documents at volume. Drop-in material for card flavour, Ghost
Front collectibles, unit bios, and anything else that needs the Office's voice.

**These are in-world artefacts** (rule 9's voice discriminator) — they speak from
inside the alternate history and name only what exists there.

---

## How to use this

Take them whole, cut them, or write more in these shapes. **The register is
fixed even when the content is not:**

- Nothing is threatened. Things are *scheduled*, *approved in part*, *held
  pending clarification*.
- Objections are **forwarded upward**, never refused.
- The most frightening sentence available is a favourable one.
- **Menace is supplied by the reader.**
- The paperwork, never the laboratory.

## 1. Requisitions and approvals

> Your requisition for four hundred (400) muzzles is approved in part. Two
> hundred are released against the current quarter. The remainder is held pending
> clarification of the discrepancy between your stated establishment and your
> feed returns, which do not reconcile.

> Approved. Note that this is the third such approval against an establishment
> that has not grown.

> Held pending clarification. Section VI has no further questions and cannot
> release against an unanswered query.

> Approved in part. The Director's countersignature is required for the
> remainder and the Director is in Breslau until Monday.

> Declined on cost. Resubmission is permitted after the quarter closes, at which
> point the cost will have changed.

> Your submission has been received and entered. Section VI refers you to the
> schedule.

## 2. Returns and abstracts

> Yield continues to improve against projection. Attrition is within tolerance
> and is, per the doctrine adopted 1942, an input rather than a loss.

> The quarterly abstract records a favourable cost variance. The Sector
> Physician's observation regarding personnel retention on this detail is
> appended at her request and is being forwarded upward.

> Establishment: as previous. Consumption: as previous. Both figures were also as
> previous in the preceding quarter and Section VI has queried which is correct.

> The feed returns and the establishment do not reconcile. The discrepancy is
> favourable. It has been favourable in every quarter since 1941.

> Throughput improvement of eleven per cent is recorded against the procedure
> adopted in March. No further explanation is appended, the procedure having been
> approved at the time.

## 3. Dispositions

> The unit remains in the field at grid reference appended. Recovery has been
> costed twice and declined twice. The occupant is entered on the establishment
> as present.

> Section VI has queried whether the occupant should be entered as *present* or
> as *equipment in the field*, since the two are drawn against different
> appropriations, and requests guidance before the next return.

> Disposition cannot proceed. The countersigning officer has been reassigned and
> the replacement declines to sign for equipment he did not receive. The item
> remains in place.

> Reissue approved. The first failure is not recorded in this file and the file
> is complete as it stands.

> Written off against the quarter as spoilage. The category is adequate to
> purpose.

## 4. Internal notes

> It has been reported that enemy troops refer to Procedure Seuche stock as
> having *risen*. The Office notes this is correct terminology and has no
> objection. The Procedure is filed under agricultural research and the
> vocabulary follows the filing. Staff are reminded that the term carries its
> ordinary baking sense and should not be embellished in correspondence.

> Personnel are reminded that the north stair is Procedure access only. Personnel
> are further reminded that this was the subject of the reminder issued in
> February.

> The estimate for further sealing is submitted for the eleventh consecutive
> year. Section VI has approved a portion.

> The Director wishes it recorded that the lunch interval is a productivity
> measure adopted on the recommendation of the Sector Physician in 1936 and is
> not a concession.

> Query from the Maritime — *no. Query from the veterinary officer regarding
> feed. Answered as previous.

## 5. The Sector Physician's objections

**Aust's nineteen.** These are the only documents at Hollernbruch written in
plain German, and the only ones nobody reads. **Never itemise all nineteen** —
the number is the point. Use fragments.

> I have written *adequate to purpose* above because that is the phrase that gets
> read. What I mean is that the seal is not adequate and I have said so three
> times.

> This is the second occasion on which I have raised this and the second occasion
> on which it has been forwarded upward. I am recording that it was the second.

> The men detailed to this duty do not re-enlist for it. I do not have a cost
> figure for that and I am aware that is why this will not be actioned.

> I am content to be overruled. I am not content to be forwarded.

## 6. Marginalia — use at one in twenty, no more
