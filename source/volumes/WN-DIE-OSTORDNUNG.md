# WERK NACHTIGALL — DIE OSTORDNUNG

**Fork B.** The thing Werk Nachtigall is downstream of.

**Read the Constitution before this file and again before writing anything from
it.** Rules 1, 2, 3 and 9 all bear directly, Volume XII's material ruling is
load-bearing, and this is the single subject in the setting where getting it
wrong is not a continuity error.

---

## 1. What it is

**Die Ostordnung — the Eastern Ordering.** Formalised as policy in 1934,
descended in a documented line from the colonial administration of the 1904–08
genocide in German South West Africa and from Ober Ost, the racialised military
regime the Empire ran in occupied Eastern Europe during the Great War.

It is the alternate history's industrialised mass murder. **It is not called the
Holocaust, it was not done by Nazis, and Auschwitz is not a place in this world**,
because none of those exist here. The Kaiser's Empire arrived at the same
destination down its own road.

Dan's framing, and it governs: *"the same bad things basically happened, just
differently."*

### It is policy, not conspiracy

**This is the most important structural fact and the easiest to get wrong.**

*Die Ostordnung* is not secret, not deniable, and not run by a shadow
organisation. It was **debated**. It has an appropriations history. It appears in
budget documents by name, in the ordinary way, alongside road-building and
harbour dredging. Ministers have defended it in sittings. There are pamphlets.

**Nobody in this world is shocked to learn it exists.** They would be shocked to
learn the figures, and the figures are not published, and not publishing them is
also a decision somebody minuted.

That is the horror and it is a *documented* horror rather than an invented one:
the real machinery was administrative, and the men running it went home at six.

## 2. How it may appear — hard rules

**These are not stylistic. They are the Constitution applied to the one subject
that most needs it.**

1. **Never a mechanic.** Rule 1. No card, no keyword, no cost, no resource, no
   objective, no location, no win condition. It does not touch the mechanical
   layer at any point, in any game, ever.
2. **Never depicted.** No scene set inside it. No camp, no transport, no killing.
   Not because it is unspeakable — because **depicting it makes it content**, and
   content is consumed.
3. **Never a source of units.** Volume XII's ruling: Werk Nachtigall's material
   is condemned criminals, prisoners of war, and the Office's own failed
   personnel. Never victims of *die Ostordnung*. **And the reason is that the
   paperwork does not permit it**, not that the Office has scruples.
4. **Its victims are never named as characters, never given bios, never
   playable.** Rule 2, absolutely.
5. **It appears only in the register of administration**, and only at the edges:
   an appropriation line, a rail allocation, a personnel transfer, a ministry
   that will not answer a query. **The paperwork, never the place.**

### What it is *for*, dramatically

**It is the thing standing behind Werk Nachtigall, permanently, unreached.**

The Office is *downstream* — in budget, in personnel, in precedent, and in the
simple fact that a state which has already decided some people are material finds
the next decision easy. Volume IV §6: **the camps come first; the programme uses
what they produce.** That ordering is the faction's moral architecture and it
must never invert.

**A story that makes the fictional horror the main event has quietly made the
real one into set dressing**, which is the failure this entire universe was built
to avoid.

So: the fungal dogs are in the foreground and they are *not the worst thing in
the world*, and the game never says so, and a player who works out what the worst
thing is has found it themselves. That is §00 pointed at the hardest possible
subject.

## 3. The register — how the Empire talks about it

This is where it may appear, and the register is the whole of it.

**Never euphemism-as-cover.** That is a thriller convention and it implies
somebody is hiding something. **Nobody is hiding this.** The euphemism is
*administrative* — the flat vocabulary of a state describing an operation it
considers legitimate and tedious.

- *Resettlement* — the word, used without inflection, in the way a ministry uses
  a word.
- *The eastern establishment* — for personnel.
- *Allocation* — for rail.
- *The ministry* — never named further. Hollernbruch's people say *the ministry*
  the way people say *the weather*.

**Nobody at Hollernbruch discusses it.** Not from fear, and not from shame.
**From irrelevance.** It is another department's appropriation and they have
their own returns to file, and this is far worse than fear would be.

### The one exception, and it is the best material in the file

**Section VI corresponds with it.** Accounts has to — there are transfers,
schedules, shared allocations.

So the only sustained contact between Werk Nachtigall and *die Ostordnung* in the
entire setting is **a series of politely worded disputes about cost**, conducted
between two offices that have never met, about a boundary neither will cross,
in a tone of mild territorial irritation.

That correspondence exists in the world. **It should never be quoted at length.**
A single line, glimpsed, is the whole thing:

> *Section VI regrets that the allocation cannot be shared as proposed and
> refers the ministry to the schedule.*

## 4. Where the games may touch it

| Game | May touch |
|---|---|
| **World Aflame** | Nothing. No card, no flavour line, no art. The word does not appear in the pool. |
| **Ghost Front** | A found document at most — an appropriation line on a form the player is reading for another reason. Never a level, never an objective, never a location. |
| **The Long Night** | Chrono reaches other eras; **it does not reach this.** A retrieval into *die Ostordnung* is forbidden outright, and the in-world reason is available and sufficient: those absences are the most heavily documented in Europe. **They fail W.D. 42-D.** |

**That last one is worth stating plainly**, because it is the point where a
well-meaning writer would reach for a rescue story: **the Consistency Finding
already forbids it.** The record is certain about these people. The programme
cannot reach them. Nobody has to invent a rule to stop the scene — the physics of
the setting stopped it before anyone asked.

And the programme has never remarked on that, because remarking would require
somebody to have checked.

---

## 5. What is deliberately absent from this file

No figures. No locations. No named victims. No description of any operation.

**That is not squeamishness and it is not silence.** It is Volume XII's principle
applied one level up: **the paperwork, never the place.** This universe says what
it has to say about *die Ostordnung* through a rail allocation and a cost dispute,
and it says it better that way than any depiction would.

If a future writer feels the absence and wants to fill it — **that impulse is the
one this file exists to refuse.**
