## 1. The signature fact: nothing here was built for this

Camp Iron Bell has the hum. Light 7 has a lamp nobody turned off. **Hollernbruch
has a building that is fighting its occupants**, and it is the single most useful
fact about the place.

A tuberculosis sanatorium is built for **light and air**. Hollernbruch has
south-facing verandas on every floor, windows eleven feet high, a solarium with a
glass roof, and a ventilation design that moves outside air through every ward
continuously, because the men who built it in 1897 believed that was medicine.

The Office needs dark, sealed, cold, and unobserved.

**So the building loses, slowly, every year, on a budget line.** Bricked windows.
Boarded verandas. Six coats of grey on the solarium glass that still admits a
green light on bright days. And the ventilation, which cannot be sealed without
condemning the structure, and which therefore **still moves air through every
ward, continuously, exactly as designed.**

That is the Seuche wing's entire security problem and the reason for the north
stair (§4).

### It is also the joke and the horror in one object

Every year the estimates carry a line for *further sealing*. Every year Section
VI approves a portion of it. **Thirteen years in, the Office is still losing an
argument with a dead architect who wanted to cure people.**

Nobody has proposed moving. Moving would require a submission.

## 2. The day

Hollernbruch runs on a schedule that has not changed since 1934.

**05:40** — first shift. The kennels are fed before the wards wake because the
noise carries.
**07:00** — theatre list posted in the main corridor, ground floor, on a board
that was the sanatorium's visiting-hours board and still has the old frame.
**07:30–13:00** — Kadaver operates. The south light is best in the morning and
the theatres are the only rooms that kept their windows (§4).
**13:00** — the whole site stops for an hour. **This is not a concession; it is
in the estimates**, because an efficiency study in 1936 found error rates rose
without it, and Section VI costed the errors.
**14:00–18:00** — second theatre list, returns, and the correspondence hour.
**18:00** — Accounts closes. Nothing can be authorised after six, which is
enforced more strictly than anything involving the wards.
**Nights** — Seuche only, and the fire watch, and Pflug, who does not keep hours
anybody set.

**The register:** Hollernbruch is not a place of screaming. It is a place of
**punctuality**. Trays on time, returns on time, lunch at one, everybody home.
The horror is not that it is chaotic. The horror is that it is *well run*.

## 3. What it smells like, and the thing nobody says

Marsh first — the drainage has never worked and the Office has costed repairing
it three times.

Then carbolic, everywhere, all year.

Then, underneath both and worst in August, **the bread smell** from the Seuche
wing (Volume IV §1). Warm, yeasty, entirely wrong.

**New personnel are told it is the bakery.** There *is* a bakery. It is four
hundred metres upwind, and anybody who thinks about the wind for ten seconds
works it out, and **nobody ever says so, and the lie is repeated to the next
intake by people who know it is a lie.**

That is the faction in a sentence. Not a conspiracy of silence — a **convention**
of it, maintained by ordinary people because correcting it would be awkward.

### The tell that matters operationally

**A Muster 6 does not smell of bread** (Volume VIII). The staff know this. It is
in no manual, and the reason it is in no manual is that writing it down would
require a document titled something nobody wants to file.

So it is taught **verbally**, on the ward, by people who learned it the same way,
and it is the only piece of genuinely life-saving knowledge at Hollernbruch that
exists entirely outside the record.

## 4. The site

- **Main building (the sanatorium).** Four floors. Kadaver on the ground, offices
  above, Section VI on the second in what was the children's ward, and **nobody
  has remarked on that in nine years.**
- **The theatres**, ground floor south. The only rooms with original windows,
  because surgical light was the one thing the Office needed that the building
  already had. **Kadaver works in daylight, with a view of the marsh.**
- **The solarium**, painted out, used for storage. The green light on bright days
  is the only beautiful thing on the site and staff eat lunch there in summer
  without ever saying why.
- **The north stair**, sealed, Seuche access only. Three doors, none of which
  close properly, all of which are in the estimates. **Grau has personally
  written the phrase "adequate to purpose" about a door he does not believe is
  adequate to purpose.**
- **The kennels**, outbuildings, downwind of the site and upwind of the village.
  This has generated correspondence since 1938 and the correspondence is still
  open.
- **The bakery.** Real. Four hundred metres upwind. Runs at four in the morning.
- **The village.** Two hundred people who have been extremely careful, for
  thirteen years, to have no opinion. The Office employs forty of them.

## 5. Sanitätsrat Dr Helene Aust

**The most important person at Hollernbruch, and the Office does not think so.**

*Prime holds what happens to her (Volume XIV). This is who she is before it.*

### The record

Forty-four. Qualified 1924, Leipzig. Came to Hollernbruch in 1936 from a
municipal hospital, on a posting she applied for, because it was a research
position and there were not many and she was good.

**She has been here eight years and has never asked to leave.** The file records
this as commitment. It is not commitment.

### What she does

She is Sector Physician: she certifies the wards, signs the returns, attends
Kadaver's list when a case is difficult, and writes the objections.

**She has filed nineteen objections in eight years.** Every one has been received,
minuted, and **forwarded upward.** None has ever come back. She knows what
forwarded upward means — everybody does — and she has continued to file them, at
a rate of roughly two a year, for eight years.

### The contradiction, which is the whole character

**She is not a dissident and she is not complicit, and both of those descriptions
are available and neither fits.**

She files objections nobody reads. She also signs the returns, attends the list,
and certifies wards she has objected to. She is the reason the lunch hour exists
in the estimates — she made the efficiency argument in 1936 **because it was the
only argument that would work**, and it worked, and people are less injured
because of it, and she got it by speaking Accounts' language rather than her own.

**That is her method and it is also her trap.** She has learned to get things by
translating them into cost, and the translation works, and every time it works it
confirms that the other language is useless.

### The eleven weeks

She was present for most of the March 1943 examination (Volume IV §3).

**She is the only person at Hollernbruch who looked at that subject as a person**
— hub edge `aust_helene -> the_march_subject REGARD 0.3`, and it is **the
strongest positive regard edge in the entire faction**, pointing from a doctor at
someone her department was taking apart.

What she actually did: she talked to them. Not interrogation — the Office had
people for that. She sat in the room during the intervals and spoke to them,
and there is no record of what was said, because **the intervals were not part
of the procedure and nothing that happens outside the procedure is written
down.**

She has not mentioned this to anyone. It is not a secret. Nobody has asked.

### Voice

Precise, unhurried, and **fluent in the Office's language to a degree that
embarrasses her.** She writes *attrition against projection* without flinching
because flinching stopped being useful in 1938.

Her objections are the only documents at Hollernbruch written in plain German,
and they are the only documents nobody reads. **She has noticed the correlation.**

### Do not

- **Do not redeem her.** Rule 7: individuals may have interiority, institutions
  get none, and she is *inside* the institution and functioning well within it.
- **Do not condemn her either.** The story does not adjudicate. §00.
- **Do not have her discover the watching.** Absolute, cross-domain, and it is
  the easiest thing in the setting to ruin. She is exactly who would work it out
  and she must never be given the chance.
