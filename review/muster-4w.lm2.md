verdict: REVISE

- **Register.** This is Hemingway and `WRITING.md` names that as the correction
  most likely to be needed. Short declaratives throughout — "Lindt takes nine."
  "The Muster's head comes up." "The Muster does not move." Terse is permitted
  at a moment of impact and nowhere else; here it is the default voice from the
  first line to the last. Rewrite toward Márquez: let the sentences run and
  accumulate, let a clause carry a history. The beat at the sluice can stay
  clipped — that is the moment of impact and it earns it.

- **No sensory texture.** The standard asks for heat and smell and cloth and
  food. There is water and there is chain and there is almost nothing else. It
  is August in drained marsh country: the dyke, the beet field and the reeds
  should smell of something, and the boy's coat is named twice without ever
  being described.

- **Missing required fields.** No `image:` and no `image_caption:`. The source
  format in `WRITING.md` requires both, and the card is a picture with a caption
  before it is anything else.

- **Two sentences carry the same load.** "It goes off the dyke into the water
  without a sound and it is across before Lindt has the chain in both hands"
  and "it has run four hundred metres in the time it takes a man to think about
  running" are the same fact told twice. Keep the second; it is better.

Not reasons to revise, recorded so the writer does not lose them:

The thesis is identifiable and it is strong — *a man saves a life by lying in
the one register the institution will accept, and the institution accepts it
because the lie is a maintenance matter.* It argues, it does not preach, and the
last line does the whole job without explaining itself. **"They look at the ears
and the ears are fine" should survive the rewrite untouched.**

The animal waiting to be told, twice on hares and once on the boy, is the card.
It is doing the work the Draw only claims.
